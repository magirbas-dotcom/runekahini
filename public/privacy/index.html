Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

Türkçe

In short: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your birth date, readings and talismans are stored only on your device. We run no server and never see this information.

This policy covers the AskRune web app (askrune.app) and the iOS/Android app, listed in the stores as "AskRune" or, in Turkish, "Rune Kahini".

1. Information stored on your device

The following information you enter or create is kept only in your device's local storage and is never sent to any server:

  • your birth date and, optionally, birth time (for the Birth Rune calculation),
  • your rune reading history, the question you typed for each and your notes,
  • birth maps kept in History: your own birth date, or one you entered for someone else, with the time if given (a partner's date entered in Rune Compatibility is never stored),
  • the talismans you saved or shared,
  • the name you chose to be greeted by (optional),
  • your latest talisman draft and the text you entered in "Write with Runes".

You can delete this at any time: your birth details with "Forget" on the Birth Rune screen, your readings, talismans and birth maps with "Clear history" on the History screen, and everything by clearing the site's or app's data or by uninstalling the app. Because it lives on your device, it does not move to another device or browser on its own.

2. Images you share

Reading cards, birth-map cards, "Write with Runes" cards and talisman wallpapers are created on your device. When you save or share one, it goes only where you send it (your photo library or the app you choose), never to us.

3. Information processed for technical reasons

  • Hosting: the web app is served by Cloudflare (and, during a transition period, Vercel). These providers briefly process technical connection data such as your IP address and browser details to deliver the page and protect it against attacks. We do not use this data to identify or track you.

Everything, fonts included, is loaded from askrune.app itself; the web app connects to no other server. We use no analytics, advertising networks, cookies or similar tracking technology.

4. Purchases in the mobile app

Subscriptions and lifetime access in the mobile app are purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Your payment details never reach us; payment and billing are governed by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies.

To verify purchases and let you restore them on other devices we use RevenueCat. RevenueCat processes an anonymous user ID created by the app, your purchase records and basic device information such as the operating system version. Your name, email address and birth details are never sent to RevenueCat. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

5. When you contact us

If you email destek@askrune.app for support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you and do not share them.

6. Children

AskRune is not directed at children under 13. As we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children.

7. Your rights

For any question about your rights under the GDPR or other data-protection laws, write to us. As the information you enter exists only on your device, you can view, correct and delete it directly in the app.

8. Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date.

9. Data controller and contact

AskRune is developed and published by Murat Ağırbaş as an individual developer, who is the data controller for the purposes of this policy.

Contact: destek@askrune.app
